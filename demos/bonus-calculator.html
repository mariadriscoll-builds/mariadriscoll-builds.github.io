What happens behind the form

The interesting part of this tool is invisible. Here is the flow the live version runs.

  1. Email first, behind a bot check

    A hidden honeypot field and a Cloudflare Turnstile check. Both are verified on the server, not in the page.

  2. A small relay, not a public form address

    The page posts to a Cloudflare Worker I wrote. The marketing platform's form address lives only in the Worker's settings, so bots can't scrape it or post to it directly.

  3. The lead is captured, and a signed ticket comes back

    The prospect is created, the autoresponder fires and sales is notified once.

  4. Their real numbers follow, quietly

    When someone stops moving the sliders for a few seconds, the page sends their figures with that ticket to a second handler that only writes fields. Sales sees the prospect's own figures, not the defaults, and nobody gets a second email.
